Restoring purchases

In Solace Tree, open Profile and tap Restore purchases; it is also at the bottom of every Premium screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. If you continued with Apple on your previous iPhone, do the same on this one first (Profile → Keep your account safe), so your purchases and cloud backup come back to the same account.

Cancelling a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Solace Tree, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge; if you cancel during a free trial, you will not be charged. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Backups and moving to a new iPhone

  • With Premium: on your current iPhone, open Profile and continue with Apple. On the new iPhone, install Solace Tree, open Profile, continue with the same Apple ID and switch to that account. Your cloud backup comes back, photos, videos and voice recordings included.
  • Without Premium: go to Profile → Backup & Restore, create a backup file (you can protect it with a password) and save it, for example to iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, restore it from the same screen. We cannot recover a forgotten backup password.
  • An iCloud or computer backup of your whole iPhone usually includes Solace Tree as well.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm. Your account, your cloud copy with its photos and recordings, and your purchase history at our payment provider are deleted, and Solace Tree’s data on your iPhone is erased. This cannot be undone. Cancel any subscription first — deleting your account does not cancel it. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is deleted.

Privacy

What you write is kept on your iPhone, encrypted. With Premium, a copy is kept in our cloud backup in the European Union; your check-ins, moods and the faith you choose never leave your iPhone. There are no ads and no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.
