What Solace Tree is, and what it is not

Solace Tree is a private place to remember someone, to write, and to get through hard days, with guided practices and information about grief. It is not medical, psychological or therapeutic care, and it does not replace a doctor, therapist or counsellor. Nobody reads what you write or watches how you are doing.

Solace Tree is not a crisis service. If you are in danger, or thinking about ending your life, call your local emergency number now (112 across Europe and in Türkiye, 911 in the United States). Crisis lines are listed in the app under Profile → Help & Support, and on our support page.
